headword

/ˈhed·wərd/ n.

The word at the head of a dictionary entry — the one you came to look up, and the one you want to use.

You know more words than you use. Headword closes that gap: one new word arrives every day on your iPhone as a full dictionary entry, and a 60-second drill has you write with it before you can say you've learned it.

Free forever: the daily word, saving words, every widget, daily notifications. Headword Plus adds 5 or 10 words a day across your hours, unlimited drills and custom collections.

The interface speaks English and Spanish; the words you learn are English. Your data stays on your device — no account, no ads, no tracking.
