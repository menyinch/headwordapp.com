# Pricing — Headword (iPhone)

Prices are the US App Store's; other countries see local prices. Billed by Apple.

## Free, forever
- Price: $0
- The daily word with its complete entry, on the Lock Screen and in the app
- Saving words and collections (Recent, by part of speech, Favorites, topics)
- Every widget: Home Screen, Lock Screen, StandBy
- Daily notifications at your hour, and an optional evening reminder
- Unlimited practice of the word on screen
- One Review session a day (spaced repetition)
- Word games: the daily grid, Guess the word, Letter by letter, Letter ring (3 free hints a level, then coins; non-personalised ads)

## Headword Plus
- Monthly: $2.99/month, 7-day free trial for eligible readers
- Yearly: $29.99/year, 7-day free trial for eligible readers
- Lifetime: $99.99 once
- Adds: 5 or 10 words a day across the hours you choose; words from topics you pick; unlimited Review; the next word whenever you want it; full word history; custom collections; past daily grids; unlimited Letter ring hints and no ads
